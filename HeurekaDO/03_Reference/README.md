# Reference Proteomics Datasets

This directory contains high-confidence baseline and perturbation proteomic surveys generated in collaboration with the laboratory of Steven Gygi (Harvard Medical School) and the Attie Laboratory. These datasets provide orthogonal, protein-level validation for transcriptomic and genetic mapping discoveries.

## Datasets

### 1. `Proteomics in 13 tissues from founder strains Steve Gygi_HEUREKA.xlsx`
- **Scope**: Comprehensive multi-tissue quantitative proteomic survey across all eight Diversity Outbred founder strains: A/J, C57BL/6J, 129S1/SvImJ, NOD/ShiLtJ, NZO/HlLtJ, CAST/EiJ, PWK/PhJ, and WSB/EiJ.
- **Tissues Profiled (13 tissues)**: Adipose (subcutaneous and visceral depots), brain (cortex, hypothalamus), heart, kidney, large intestine, liver, lung, pancreas, skeletal muscle (gastrocnemius, soleus), and small intestine.
- **Application**: Establishes baseline strain-specific protein abundance, baseline genetic regulation of protein expression, and cross-tissue protein distribution profiles.

### 2. `Liver proteomics in founder diet study unaware of coding variants Gygi_HEUREKA.csv`
- **Scope**: Large-scale quantitative liver proteomics (~124 MB) assessing hepatic proteome remodeling across founder strains under experimental dietary interventions (chow vs. high-fat / high-sucrose diet).
- **Design Note**: Quantified using peptide spectral matching unaware of founder coding variants (ensuring unskewed baseline peptide detection across diverse genetic backgrounds).

### 3. `Proteomics of mouse islets following overnight treatment with HSP90beta inhibitor_HEUREKA.xlsx`
- **Scope**: Targeted pharmacological perturbation of isolated mouse pancreatic islets treated overnight with an HSP90beta inhibitor.
- **Application**: Investigates molecular chaperoning, beta-cell stress pathways, and protein folding homeostasis relevant to diabetes pathogenesis.
